2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Where is my artwork stored? Is it uploaded to the cloud?

PPaint works 100% offline. All of your artworks, layers, custom brushes, and app settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is ever uploaded to any cloud service or external server. Before uninstalling the app, please export your artworks to back them up.

Q2: How do I locate and use the "AR face preview" feature?

The "AR face preview" feature maps your 2D canvas drawing onto a 3D face mesh in real time using Apple's TrueDepth camera APIs to help you design face paint, makeup, and facial stickers. To locate and use it:

  1. Open PPaint, then open an existing artwork or tap + (Create) in the Gallery to enter the canvas workspace (a square canvas such as 2048 × 2048 is recommended).
  2. In the top toolbar of the canvas workspace, tap the ••• (Actions) button on the top-left side.
  3. Under the Assist section of the menu, tap AR face preview.
  4. Tap Generate UV template layer to insert a 2D face mesh UV wireframe layer onto your canvas as a painting guide (this layer is undoable and can be hidden or deleted anytime).
  5. Tap Continue and allow camera use (or Start preview) to start live face tracking with the front-facing TrueDepth camera and preview your canvas artwork mapped onto your face in real time (background color is excluded so unpainted areas show your real face).

Q3: Why does it say "This device does not support face tracking"?

"AR face preview" requires an iPhone or iPad equipped with a front-facing TrueDepth camera (Face ID). On devices without a TrueDepth camera, all regular painting and editing tools remain fully functional.

Q4: Which file formats are supported for import and export?

  • Artworks & Brushes: Native .ppaint artworks, .ppbrushes brush packs, and compatible third-party brush and swatch formats.
  • Images & Video: Import and export for PSD (with layers preserved), PNG, JPEG, and TIFF images, as well as timelapse video export (MP4 / MOV).

Q5: What if I denied Camera or Photos permissions?

Painting and saving artworks locally do not require any system permissions. If you later wish to save exported images to your Photo Library or use the "AR face preview" feature, you can enable access anytime in Settings → Apps → PPaint.

3. Privacy Policy & TrueDepth Camera Data Disclosure

PPaint is built with strict privacy protection by design:

  • Zero Data Collection & 100% Offline: PPaint contains no networking code, no third-party analytics, and no advertising SDKs. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user content.
  • Photo Library Access: Add-only Photo Library permission is requested only when you explicitly choose to save an exported artwork or video to your device's Photos app. PPaint never reads your existing photo library.
  • TrueDepth API & Face Data Disclosure:
    • What Data is Accessed & Purpose: On supported devices with a TrueDepth camera, PPaint uses Apple's ARKit (ARFaceTrackingConfiguration, ARSCNFaceGeometry, and ARFaceGeometry) solely when you open the optional AR face preview feature (Workspace → ••• (Actions) → Assist → AR face preview). This data is used exclusively to generate a 2D UV wireframe template layer on the canvas and to render your 2D canvas drawing as a live 3D texture mapped onto your face mesh in real time.
    • Storage & Retention: All camera frames and face mesh geometry data are processed ephemerally in real-time memory on the device only. Face data is never recorded, never written to disk, and never retained. The AR camera session pauses and stops immediately as soon as you close the "AR face preview" sheet or switch the app to the background.
    • Third-Party Sharing: No camera images or face data are ever transmitted off the device or shared with any third parties.
